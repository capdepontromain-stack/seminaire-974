Your incentive or corporate seminar in Reunion Island

A local agency, real published prices, one point of contact

Reunion Island sits 45 minutes by air from Mauritius, and it is unlike anywhere else in the Indian Ocean: an active volcano, three UNESCO-listed mountain cirques, a lagoon on the west coast, and a Creole culture that turns a corporate event into a story people keep telling. For a company or a MICE agency based in Mauritius, South Africa or India, it is the rare destination that feels far away while staying remarkably simple to work with. This page explains why, and what we do here.

Why Reunion Island reassures your finance team

A French overseas department

Reunion is France in the Indian Ocean: EU territory, euro currency, French law and French invoicing. Political stability, European health and safety standards, reliable infrastructure. The exotic setting without the country risk.

Scenery Mauritius does not have

Piton de la Fournaise, one of the world's most active volcanoes. The cirques of Mafate, Cilaos and Salazie. Waterfalls, black sand, lagoon. Your programme changes landscape every day without changing hotels.

Easy from the region

Direct flights of about 45 minutes from Mauritius with Air Austral and Air Mauritius, several rotations a day, plus long-haul links to Roland Garros airport (RUN). Same time zone as Mauritius, minimal jet lag from India and South Africa.

Transparent pricing

We are the only event agency on the island that publishes its real prices online. You can benchmark us before you even call, and every quote is itemised line by line.

Who we are, in numbers

We have delivered more than 500 events on the island and hold a 4.9/5 rating on Google, based on 111 public reviews.   • Venues: lagoon-side resorts, Creole estates, equipped conference rooms, exceptional private venues. We work with these places all year and know their real availability.
  • Logistics: airport transfers, shuttles between sites, rooming lists, catering with local caterers, schedule management.

  • Coordination: one contact from brief to debrief, on site on the day, from the first transfer to the last departure.

Budget: honest numbers first

Our published French-language budget page gives the framework, and it applies to international groups too: on-site residential seminar costs run from about €280 to €380 per person per day (conference room, full catering, accommodation, activities), and our agency fee for programme design, entertainment and coordination runs from €1,050 excl. VAT for a short session to about €12,000 excl. VAT for a large full-day turnkey event. Team building alone runs from €760 to €4,240 excl. VAT depending on group size.

About VAT: all prices are quoted excl. VAT. French VAT applies at the local rate of 8.5% for services delivered in Reunion Island. VAT treatment for foreign companies depends on your situation: invoicing details on request, itemised in the quote.

When to come, and for how long

The best season runs from May to November: the southern winter brings dry weather, around 25°C on the coast, no cyclones, open hiking trails, and humpback whales visible from the west coast between June and October. Three to four days on the island is the format that works: real work sessions in the morning, discovery and team building in the afternoon, a Creole evening to close. Many groups extend the trip into a two-island programme: our Reunion + Mauritius combo page covers that format.

Reunion is a French overseas department: EU territory, euro currency, French law, European safety standards and French invoicing. Add scenery no neighbouring island can match, an active volcano, UNESCO-listed cirques and a lagoon, and you get a destination that is both spectacular and administratively simple.

From Mauritius, direct flights of about 45 minutes with Air Austral and Air Mauritius, several times a day.
