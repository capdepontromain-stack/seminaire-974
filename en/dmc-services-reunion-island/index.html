What we handle on the island

Our value for a client 5,000 kilometres away fits in one sentence: everything that must physically happen in Reunion Island goes through us.

  • Venues: sourcing, negotiation and booking. Lagoon-side resorts, Creole estates, equipped conference rooms, exceptional private venues. We work with them all year and know their real availability and their hidden constraints.
  • Local logistics: airport transfers, shuttles between sites, rooming lists, catering with the island's caterers, schedule management.
  • Entertainment: our historic core business, delivered in-house. Animators, MCs, DJs, team building (beach olympics, survivor-style challenges, treasure hunts), Creole evenings.
  • Technical production: sound, microphones, screens, branded lighting, video capture.
  • Coordination: one contact from brief to debrief, on the ground on the day, from the first transfer to the last departure.

How we work with an agency

  1. You keep the client relationship. Your agency designs the offer, sells and invoices as the deal requires. We work directly or in support of your agency, whichever serves the file best, and the exact framework is agreed at the first call.
  2. Brief by video call, proposal within 24 hours. Headcount, dates, target budget: we come back with a programme and an itemised quote. Our price ranges are already published online, which lets you frame your client before you even contact us.
  3. Remote scouting. Video visits of shortlisted venues, real photos and floor plans, no embellished catalogue.
  4. Execution and on-site presence. On the day, our teams are on the ground. If your project manager travels, they lead and we execute; if not, we report continuously.
  5. Debrief. A factual post-event report, photos and material for your client reporting.
