Reunion + Mauritius: one seminar, two islands

45 minutes apart by air, one schedule, one coordinator

Reunion Island and Mauritius are the most complementary pair in the Indian Ocean: on one side the volcano, the mountain cirques and the Creole energy of Reunion, on the other the beaches and resort hotels of Mauritius. A two-island corporate seminar plays to both strengths: the substance in Reunion, the reward in Mauritius. Here is how to build it without drowning in inter-island logistics.

Flights and formalities, in plain terms

  • The flight. The Reunion-Mauritius route is the busiest regional link in the zone, flown by Air Austral and Air Mauritius with several daily rotations, about 45 minutes in the air. For a group of 20 or more on the same rotation, book early and fix flight times before locking the programme, not the other way round.
  • Passports. Mauritius is outside the European Union: every participant needs a valid passport for the Mauritian leg. Check this at registration, not three weeks before departure. For the Reunion leg alone, EU citizens can travel on a national ID card; other nationalities need a passport and, depending on citizenship, a French visa.
  • One schedule. Two hotels, two sets of suppliers and a flight in the middle: without a single pilot, friction piles up. We organise the Reunion side directly and coordinate the Mauritian leg with the hotel or local DMC of your choice, on one master schedule.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the flight between Reunion and Mauritius?

About 45 minutes, on a route flown by Air Austral and Air Mauritius with several flights a day. It is the simplest inter-island transfer in the Indian Ocean.

Do participants need a passport?

Yes for Mauritius, which is outside the European Union: a valid passport is required for every participant. For the Reunion leg alone, EU citizens can travel on a national ID card; other nationalities need a passport and, depending on citizenship, a French visa.

Who organises the Mauritian leg?

We pilot the overall schedule and coordinate the Mauritian leg with the hotel or local DMC you choose in Mauritius: one contact for both islands, transfers and timings aligned on each side. The Reunion leg is delivered directly by our own teams.
